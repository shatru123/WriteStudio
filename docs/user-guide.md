# WriteStudio User Guide

Welcome to **WriteStudio**! This guide walks you through setting up and recording your first teaching lecture.

## Step 1: Connect Devices
1. Plug in your USB microphone (e.g. Blue Yeti, Rode, Elgato Wave).
2. Connect your graphics tablet / drawing pad (e.g. Wacom, Huion, XP-Pen, Apple Pencil/Sidecar, or Surface Pen).
3. Connect your webcam if you wish to record a presenter video layer.

## Step 2: Load Reference Slides (Private)
1. In the left panel, click **"Load Slides (PDF / Image)..."**.
2. Select your lecture slides (PDF, PNG, JPG).
3. Use the **◀ Prev** and **Next ▶** buttons to navigate through your slides during your lecture.
4. *Remember*: These slides are private to you and will **never** appear in the exported video!

## Step 3: Draw & Teach
1. Select your drawing tool from the right palette: **Pen**, **Pencil**, **Highlighter**, **Shapes**, **Text**, or **Eraser**.
2. Pick colors from the palette.
3. Switch canvas background to **Blackboard**, **Grid**, or **Ruled Paper** to suit your subject (Math, Physics, Programming).
4. Add new pages using **➕ Add Page** when you fill the current canvas.

## Step 4: Record & Export
1. Click **● RECORD** in the bottom bar.
2. Watch the live audio VU meter to ensure your microphone levels are healthy.
3. Use **❚❚ PAUSE** and **▶ RESUME** if you need a quick break. Paused time is automatically deducted so your final video contains no awkward dead air.
4. Click **◼ STOP** when finished.
5. Click **🎬 Export MP4 Video...**, pick your resolution (1080p / 720p / 4K) and frame rate (30 / 60 FPS), and click **Start Export**.
